The world of our game is semi-realistic. Contrary to our world, the animals here possess higher levels of sentience. They also have the fictional ability to communicate in language with each other. However, we do not include animals or plants that do not exist in the real world.

The world that our beaver wakes up in is a world that has suffered a severe drought. As such, life has fled the once lush forest. Luckily a tiny oasis survived allowing our hero to bootstrap the local environment back into prosperity.

The first other animal that The Beaver encounters is The Fox. The Fox is a mischievous little fella’. While helpful in some regards. His kleptomania is certainly to our hero’s great dismay. The Beaver quickly discovers that one can’t leave items unguarded for too long with The Fox around.

Undeterred by the pranks of The Fox, our hero moves on to bigger matters. His dam rejuvenates the local area. Attracting a variety of wildlife back into the ecosystem. Chief among them is The Elk, who using his big antlers scares away The Fox whenever he gets too rousey. But of course, having such a large guardian by your side has a cost that comes with it. A giant must eat, and so The Beaver must remain busy in order to ensure that there is not only enough for all the animals to eat, but also enough to last everyone during the winter.

While tough, the work ends up worth it as by around the third year, The Beaver is able to create a forest inviting to woodland critters, big and small, from all over the local area. Until one day The Beaver realizes that his work there is done.
